How it works

Three taps from empty to tracking.

Start with a few ready-made habits, or write your own. Nothing to configure first.

  1. Add a habit

    Name it, pick a daily target such as 30 pages or 45 minutes, and choose the days it happens. More options like colour and a goal are one tap away.

  2. Check it off

    Tap a day to mark the full target done. Hold it to enter an exact amount, so a half session still counts as progress.

  3. Watch it add up

    Streaks, weekly summaries, a month calendar and a year view update as you go. Hit 7, 30 or 100 days and the app notices.

What you get

Made for daily use.

Targets with units

Minutes, pages, glasses, meals. Set the amount that counts as done and log partial progress along the way.

Custom schedules

Weekdays only, weekends, or any days you pick. Rest days are supported and handled consistently.

Calendar for each habit

Open any habit to see its month, jump back through earlier weeks, and add a note to a day.

Calm, short animations

Check-offs, windows and menus move quickly and stay out of the way. They switch to simple fades if your phone asks for reduced motion.

Privacy

Your habits are yours.

  • No account. Open it and start. There is no email or password to give.
  • No server holds your data. Everything is stored in your browser on your own device.
  • You hold the backup. Save a file whenever you like, and restore it if you switch phones.
  • Clearing your browser data removes your habits, so save a backup first. The app reminds you.

Put it on your home screen

Use it like an app

  1. Open habitflow-offline.vercel.app in Chrome while you're online.
  2. Open Chrome's menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open it once from the new icon. After that it also opens without signal.
